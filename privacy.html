Privacy Policy

Idle Rift Driver

Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short

Idle Rift Driver does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details. Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your Google account.

If you choose to allow analytics, the game sends gameplay and technical data to Unity Analytics as described below. This data is not associated with a player account or with contact details provided to the game.

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. To check that a purchase is genuine, the game sends its purchase token to a verification service run by the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, including opened areas, upgrade levels, resources, quest and turret state, and your selected language, is stored locally on your device. The game does not intentionally send this save data to the developer or to Unity Analytics.

Local game data is normally removed when you uninstall the game, subject to your device and platform backup or restore settings.

Google Play Games and cloud saves

If Google Play Games is set up on your device, the game signs you in to it automatically. Google Play Games shares your gamer identity (gamer name and avatar) with the game. The game uses the sign-in only to keep a copy of your save.

While you are signed in, a copy of your game progress is uploaded to Google Play Games Saved Games every ten minutes of play, and when you press the save button on the Saves screen. Google stores the copy in your Google account; it is not kept on a server run by the developer. The copy is used to restore your progress on another device or after a reinstall. You can turn the automatic upload off on the Saves screen.

You can delete the game’s Play Games data, including the cloud save, in your Play Games profile settings or your Google Account: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646

Time check

To measure how long you were away from the game, it asks a Google server (www.google.com) for the current time when it starts and when you return to it. Only the time is read from the answer; the request carries no game data.

Analytics data

If you consent to analytics, the game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics. These may include events such as session start and end, opening an area, purchasing an upgrade, defeating a guardian, resetting progress, opening the shop, buying a pack, and similar gameplay actions. Events may include game-related values such as an area number or upgrade level.

These analytics events are not associated with your name, email address, phone number, or a player account in Idle Rift Driver.

Unity Analytics may also process technical information about your device and app session, such as device model and manufacturer, operating system version, language, an installation identifier, and approximate location derived from your network connection. Idle Rift Driver does not request precise GPS location.

The installation identifier is used to distinguish installations for analytics purposes and is not linked by the game to an account or contact information.

For more information about how Unity handles data, see Unity's privacy policy.

Consent and analytics settings

When the game first asks for analytics permission, you can choose whether to allow analytics collection. If you decline, the game is intended not to send optional gameplay analytics events.

You can change your analytics choice in the game settings at any time. Changes take effect for future analytics collection.

Children

Idle Rift Driver is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 through the game.

In-app purchases

Idle Rift Driver offers optional one-time purchases, processed by Google Play. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Google and are never seen by the game or the developer.

When you make a purchase, the game sends the purchase token and the product identifier to a verification service run by the developer on Google Cloud. The service asks Google Play whether the purchase is genuine, confirms it to Google Play, and returns the result to the game. The service does not store the token and does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Google Cloud may keep standard request logs, such as the time of a request and the network address it came from, for a limited period.

If you consent to analytics, the game also reports the steps of a purchase to Unity Analytics: which pack, whether it was on sale, whether the purchase went through or why it did not, and how long the check took. These events do not contain the purchase token or any payment details.

The packs you buy are also recorded in your local save, so they work offline. Google Play remains the record of what you own, and the game restores your packs from it after a reinstall.

For how Google handles payments, see Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The current version of Idle Rift Driver contains no advertising.

The developer does not sell personal data. Analytics data is processed through Unity Analytics as described in this policy.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features, analytics practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when the policy changes.
