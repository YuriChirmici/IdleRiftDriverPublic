Privacy Policy

Idle Rift Driver

Last updated: 22 September 2026

In short

Idle Rift Driver does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

If you choose to allow analytics, the game sends gameplay and technical data to Unity Analytics as described below. This data is not associated with a player account or with contact details provided to the game.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, including opened areas, upgrade levels, resources, quest and turret state, and your selected language, is stored locally on your device. The game does not intentionally send this save data to the developer or to Unity Analytics.

Local game data is normally removed when you uninstall the game, subject to your device and platform backup or restore settings.

Analytics data

If you consent to analytics, the game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics. These may include events such as session start and end, opening an area, purchasing an upgrade, defeating a guardian, resetting progress, and similar gameplay actions. Events may include game-related values such as an area number or upgrade level.

These analytics events are not associated with your name, email address, phone number, or a player account in Idle Rift Driver.

Unity Analytics may also process technical information about your device and app session, such as device model and manufacturer, operating system version, language, an installation identifier, and approximate location derived from your network connection. Idle Rift Driver does not request precise GPS location.

The installation identifier is used to distinguish installations for analytics purposes and is not linked by the game to an account or contact information.

For more information about how Unity handles data, see Unity's privacy policy.

Consent and analytics settings

When the game first asks for analytics permission, you can choose whether to allow analytics collection. If you decline, the game is intended not to send optional gameplay analytics events.

You can change your analytics choice in the game settings at any time. Changes take effect for future analytics collection.

Children

Idle Rift Driver is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 through the game.

Advertising and purchases

The current version of Idle Rift Driver contains no advertising.

The developer does not sell personal data. Analytics data is processed through Unity Analytics as described in this policy.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features, analytics practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when the policy changes.
